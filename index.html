Garett is an entrepreneur and multi-disciplinary business leader with engineering and product roots. At heart, he is a builder and connector who loves to create. In the beginning, he built with Legos. Then he discovered software and wrote applications to connect people, help them communicate, and have fun online.

Garett's professional experiences and expertise are broad and deep spanning leadership roles at Meta, Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft. Recently, he founded and led the Value Share business unit at Flow, an early-stage real estate startup. Currently, he's a Founding Partner at Resilient Networks, a novel connectivity start-up making Way2.

Garett is passionate about advancing equitable and humane technology and the power of human-centric networks.
